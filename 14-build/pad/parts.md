The spec's §13 "Pad" line was $116 including HALO's float. The difference is the CNC-turned nails (C2 profile), the bigger SLA deck and the services.

**Cheaper option for the nails:** turn them yourself from Ø 4 mm POM rod in a cordless drill with a file and the PD17 card. It saves ≈ $80 but needs home iteration (≈ 2 h for 12 nails). Tolerance on the land then depends on the stock rod: Ø 4.0 rod usually measures 3.95–4.05, so pick rods that pass the Ø 3.95 slot.
