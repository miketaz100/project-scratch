Deliver an ON-PAR SCRATCHING SENSATION: as satisfying as being scratched well by a person. It does NOT have to feel like a human hand. It must still be a scratch (edge reaching the scalp, sliding), not a massage, vibration or brush. Anti-habituation variation still matters.
