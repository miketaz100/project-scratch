# PORCUPINE HELMET — architecture brief (Michael's direction, 2026-10-01)

## The architecture to design for
A full-coverage head-worn SCRATCHING helmet built from one multiplied unit:
- OUTER SHELL on a hard-hat ratchet suspension (skull-registered, light, open where possible).
- INNER SHELL (carrier) that moves relative to the outer shell by 25–35 mm strokes / small orbits, driven by TWO servos, following the skull's curvature.
- PINS: dozens of thin antenna-like pins mounted on the inner shell, each ending in a NAIL TIP on a short FLEXIBLE NECK (self-aligning attack angle, no joint near hair), each spring-loaded outward with a soft, long-travel spring so scalp force is ~constant despite seating slop, each parked RETRACTED by a latch so it does not touch the head.
- SELECTOR: one slow motor (cam disc / rotating band / stepping bar) that releases 3–5 pins at a time; the released group extends, rides the moving inner shell, and scratches; the active group "walks" around the head on a pattern. Only a hand's worth of contacts is active at any moment (sensation model §7: 3–5 contacts; dozens at once = brush/massage; ≤ 12 N total on the head).
- Lift-off at reversals comes from the inner-shell path (geometric/cam) or from momentary retraction.
- Firmware: pattern engine (stroke length/speed/timing jitter, episodes, group wander), hold-to-run, e-stop, current limits.

## SP1 under this architecture
SP1 = ONE CROWN SECTOR of the helmet: a ~120 × 100 mm curved panel carrying 8–12 pins, the real 2-servo shell drive, the real selector, on a hard-hat suspension, with the real firmware. Everything in SP1 must be the helmet's actual design, so that the full helmet is replication, not redesign. Target ≤ 300 g on the head for the sector, ≤ 60 mm tall above the scalp over the sector.
